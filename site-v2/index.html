Our Services

From emergency restoration to home remodeling, we restore and rebuild properties across Central Indiana.

Water Damage Restoration

Burst pipes, flooding, or sewage backup — we respond fast to extract water, dry your property, and restore damaged structures and contents to pre-loss condition.

Learn More

Fire Damage Restoration

After a fire, we handle board-up, water removal from firefighting efforts, soot and smoke cleanup, odor removal, and full structural repairs to get you back home.

Learn More

Mold Remediation

Mold growth needs attention to both the affected materials and the moisture source. We identify the source, contain the affected area, remove mold safely, and treat surfaces to prevent regrowth.

Learn More

Storm Damage Restoration

Wind, hail, heavy rain, and fallen trees can cause serious damage. We provide emergency tarping, water extraction, structural drying, and complete restoration.

Learn More

Home Remodeling

Bathroom and kitchen remodels, full-home renovations, and rebuild services. Whether after a disaster or a planned upgrade, we deliver quality craftsmanship.

Learn More

Handyman Repairs

Drywall repair, painting, power washing, deck and fence work, gutter cleaning, and general maintenance. Our trusted team handles it all — insured and bonded.

Why Choose Becht Pride?

When disaster strikes, you need a team that responds fast and restores your home right. Becht Pride Restoration serves homeowners across Central Indiana — from emergency water and fire damage restoration to complete home remodeling.

We handle the full process: emergency response, damage mitigation, cleanup, and reconstruction. One company from start to finish means faster recovery, lower costs, and less stress. We also work directly with your insurance company to navigate the claims process.
