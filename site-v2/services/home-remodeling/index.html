Home Remodeling for your home

Plan a more useful, comfortable home with Becht Pride. Our Indianapolis home remodeling services include full-home renovations, basement finishing, flooring, interior updates, and reconstruction after water, fire, mold, or storm damage.

A larger renovation works best with a clear scope. We discuss the rooms involved, the layout you want to keep or change, and the finishes that matter to you. Our team handles the coordination between stages so you can understand how demolition, construction, installation, and finishing fit together.

What’s included

  • Full-home renovations and room updates
  • Basement finishing and flooring installation
  • Drywall, painting, trim, and finish carpentry
  • Kitchen and bathroom renovation coordination
  • Post-disaster reconstruction after restoration

Our process

  1. Discuss your goals

    We begin with a free consultation about the existing home, your priorities, and the work you want to include.

  2. Define the estimate

    We discuss materials, scope, access, and the project sequence before work begins. Changes to the plan are discussed as the work develops.

  3. Build and review

    Our team coordinates the renovation and reviews the finished work with you, including any remaining details.

Reconstruction after property damage

Our restoration and remodeling teams connect cleanup with rebuilding. Once affected areas are ready, we can repair or replace drywall, flooring, cabinets, trim, and other finishes. We discuss the repair scope and any optional upgrades separately, including how to keep insurance-related repairs distinct from planned improvements.

Tell us about the loss and the rooms involved so the assessment and estimate can account for the complete project.

Insurance help and repair estimates

For work connected to an insured property loss, we can document the proposed repairs and provide an estimate for your adjuster. Routine maintenance and planned improvements are quoted as their own projects. Coverage depends on your policy and is decided by your insurer.

Home Remodeling: common questions

Do you offer free remodeling estimates?

Yes. Becht Pride offers a free consultation and estimate for remodeling projects. Tell us about the rooms, goals, and priorities you want to discuss.

Can you finish a basement?

Yes. Basement finishing is part of our home remodeling services. We discuss the existing conditions and proposed use of the space when developing the project scope.

Do you rebuild after water or fire damage?

Yes. Our restoration team handles cleanup and mitigation, and our remodeling services cover reconstruction and replacement finishes after the affected areas are ready.

Can I change the layout during a restoration project?

We can discuss layout changes and improvements alongside the repair plan. Optional upgrades should be priced and agreed separately from damage-related repairs, particularly when insurance is involved.

How long will my home renovation take?

The schedule depends on the scope, material availability, and conditions uncovered during work. We discuss sequencing and timing for your project rather than promise a standard completion date.
