Water Damage Restoration for your home

Water in your home needs a plan that connects cleanup, drying, and repairs. Becht Pride Restoration answers emergency calls 24/7 and helps Indianapolis homeowners recover from plumbing leaks, basement water, appliance failures, and storm-related intrusion.

Water can move beneath flooring and into wall cavities before the full extent of a loss is visible. We assess the affected materials, use extraction and drying equipment, and discuss the repairs needed to return the space to use. Our remodeling team can continue the work through drywall, flooring, trim, and other finishes.

What’s included

  • Emergency water extraction and assessment of affected rooms
  • Structural drying and moisture monitoring
  • Cleanup of damaged surfaces and assessment of belongings
  • Sewage-related cleanup with the appropriate containment and handling
  • Reconstruction after drying, including drywall and flooring

Our process

  1. Assess the loss

    Tell us where water appeared, whether the source is still active, and which rooms are affected. We assess access, materials, and the scope of work.

  2. Extract and dry

    We remove standing water and set up a drying plan appropriate to the materials and conditions, monitoring progress before enclosing repaired areas.

  3. Repair and restore

    We discuss the documented repair scope and coordinate reconstruction, keeping optional improvements separate from necessary repairs.

Insurance help and repair estimates

We can document the damage, provide repair estimates, and communicate with your adjuster about the scope of work. Your insurer determines policy coverage, deductibles, and payment. Optional upgrades should be discussed separately from the damage-related repair estimate.

Water Damage Restoration: common questions

Do you answer water damage calls at night?

Yes. Becht Pride answers emergency restoration calls 24/7. Call (463) 238-4357 to explain the situation and confirm the next steps and crew availability.

What should I do before the restoration team arrives?

Keep people and pets away from standing water and damaged electrical equipment. Do not enter an unsafe structure. For immediate danger, call 911. Share photos only if they can be taken from a safe location.

Can you repair the home after water cleanup?

Yes. Our restoration and remodeling services connect extraction and drying with repairs such as drywall, flooring, trim, and room reconstruction.

How long does drying take?

Drying time depends on the materials, the amount of moisture, and the conditions in the home. We assess and monitor the affected areas rather than promise the same timeline for every loss.

Will insurance pay for water damage restoration?

Coverage depends on your policy and the cause of the damage. We can document the loss and provide repair estimates; your insurance company determines coverage and payment.
