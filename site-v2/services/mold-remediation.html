Professional Mold Remediation in Indianapolis

Mold is more than an eyesore — it's a serious health hazard that can compromise your family's well-being and your home's structural integrity. Mold spores are always present in the air, but when they find moisture, they can colonize rapidly, spreading behind walls, under flooring, and through HVAC systems in as little as 24 to 48 hours. Becht Pride Restoration and Remodeling provides thorough, professional mold remediation throughout Indianapolis and Central Indiana.

Indianapolis homeowners face elevated mold risks due to the region's humid summers, frequent rain, and older housing stock with aging plumbing. Common causes of indoor mold growth include undetected water leaks, poor ventilation in bathrooms and kitchens, basement moisture intrusion, and lingering damage from floods or burst pipes. If you've recently experienced water damage of any kind, mold inspection should be a priority.

Exposure to mold can cause a range of health problems, particularly for children, the elderly, and individuals with respiratory conditions or compromised immune systems. Symptoms may include persistent coughing, sneezing, eye irritation, skin rashes, headaches, and in severe cases, chronic respiratory illness. Black mold (Stachybotrys chartarum) is especially dangerous and requires professional remediation — never attempt to remove it yourself.

Our mold remediation process is thorough and systematic. We don't just remove visible mold — we identify the moisture source causing the growth and address it to prevent recurrence. Using containment barriers, negative air pressure, HEPA filtration, and EPA-registered antimicrobial agents, we eliminate mold colonies while preventing spores from spreading to unaffected areas of your home.

After remediation is complete, we repair and restore any areas that were damaged during the removal process. This may include replacing drywall, insulation, flooring, or trim. Because Becht Pride handles both remediation and reconstruction, you get a seamless experience with a single contractor from start to finish.

Our Mold Remediation Process

  • Inspection & Assessment — Thorough visual inspection and moisture testing to identify mold growth and its source
  • Containment — Physical barriers and negative air pressure prevent mold spores from spreading during remediation
  • Air Filtration — HEPA air scrubbers and vacuums capture microscopic mold spores from the air
  • Mold Removal — Safe removal of mold-contaminated materials including drywall, insulation, and carpet
  • Cleaning & Sanitization — Antimicrobial treatments applied to all affected surfaces and belongings
  • Moisture Source Repair — Fixing the underlying water issue that caused the mold growth
  • Restoration & Reconstruction — Replacing removed materials and restoring your home to pre-loss condition
  • Prevention Guidance — Recommendations for humidity control, ventilation, and monitoring to prevent future mold

If you suspect mold in your home — whether you can see it, smell it, or are experiencing unexplained health symptoms — don't wait. Mold spreads quickly and the longer it grows, the more extensive and costly the remediation becomes.
