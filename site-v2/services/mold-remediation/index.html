Mold Remediation for your home

Mold cleanup should address the moisture problem as well as the affected materials. Becht Pride provides mold remediation for Indianapolis and Central Indiana homeowners, with assessment, containment, removal, and repair planning based on the conditions in the home.

Tell us where growth or a musty odor appeared and whether the area has a history of leaking, flooding, or condensation. The scope depends on the size of the affected area, the materials involved, and the source of moisture. Painting over a damp or mold-affected surface does not resolve the underlying problem.

What’s included

  • Inspection of visible affected areas and moisture conditions
  • Containment appropriate to the work area
  • Removal or cleaning of affected materials as appropriate
  • Discussion of moisture correction and recurrence prevention
  • Drywall, flooring, or finish repairs after remediation

Our process

  1. Understand the moisture

    We assess the visible conditions and discuss leaks, water intrusion, or other moisture issues that may need to be addressed.

  2. Contain and remediate

    The work plan identifies the area to be contained and the materials to be removed or cleaned.

  3. Restore the space

    Once the remediation and moisture-related work are addressed, we discuss replacement finishes and any additional repairs.

Insurance help and repair estimates

We can document the damage, provide repair estimates, and communicate with your adjuster about the scope of work. Your insurer determines policy coverage, deductibles, and payment. Optional upgrades should be discussed separately from the damage-related repair estimate.

Mold Remediation: common questions

Does mold remediation include fixing the moisture problem?

Moisture must be addressed for remediation to be effective. We discuss the likely source and the work required; the exact repair scope depends on the cause and the property.

Should I paint over visible mold?

No. Covering an affected surface does not remove mold or correct the moisture that supports it. Have the condition assessed before repainting or enclosing the area.

Do I need a mold test before calling?

You can call to discuss visible growth, odors, or water damage without already having a test. We can explain the assessment process and discuss whether additional testing is appropriate for your situation.

Can you replace drywall after remediation?

Yes. Our remodeling and repair services can include replacement drywall, flooring, trim, and other finishes after the affected area is ready for reconstruction.

Is mold damage covered by homeowners insurance?

That depends on the policy and circumstances of the loss. Ask your insurer about coverage. We can document affected materials and provide an estimate for the proposed work.
