Storm Damage Restoration in Indianapolis

Indiana is no stranger to severe weather. From the powerful thunderstorms that roll through every spring and summer to the occasional tornado, ice storm, and straight-line wind event, Central Indiana homeowners know that storm damage is not a matter of "if" but "when." When a storm damages your home, Becht Pride Restoration and Remodeling is here with fast, professional restoration to get your life back to normal.

Storm damage can take many forms — a tree crashing through your roof, hail shattering windows and destroying siding, high winds ripping off shingles, or flash flooding pouring water into your basement. Each type of damage requires a different approach, and our experienced team has the training and equipment to handle all of them. We respond to storm emergencies 24/7, because storms don't wait for business hours and neither do we.

The first priority after a storm is securing your property to prevent further damage. Our crews arrive equipped for emergency tarping of damaged roofs, board-up of broken windows and doors, and temporary structural bracing. These immediate measures protect your home from additional rain, wind, and unauthorized entry while we develop a comprehensive restoration plan.

Once your property is secured, our team conducts a detailed damage assessment. We document everything — from obvious structural damage to hidden issues like moisture intrusion behind walls, compromised insulation, and damaged electrical systems. This thorough documentation is essential for your insurance claim and ensures nothing is missed during the restoration process.

Central Indiana's storm season can be unpredictable. The Indianapolis metro area sits in the heart of the Midwest, where warm, moist air from the Gulf meets cold fronts from the north, creating conditions ripe for severe thunderstorms, damaging hail, and tornadoes. Whether your home was hit by a summer supercell or a winter ice storm, Becht Pride has the local expertise to restore it properly.

Our Storm Damage Services

  • Emergency Tarping & Board-Up — Immediate protection for damaged roofs, windows, and openings
  • Tree & Debris Removal — Safe removal of fallen trees, branches, and storm debris from your property
  • Water Extraction & Structural Drying — Removing storm water and thoroughly drying affected areas to prevent mold
  • Wind Damage Repair — Repairing or replacing siding, roofing, gutters, and trim damaged by high winds
  • Hail Damage Restoration — Addressing dented siding, cracked shingles, broken windows, and surface damage
  • Roof Damage Repair — From minor shingle replacement to complete re-roofing after severe damage
  • Structural Repairs — Rebuilding walls, framing, and load-bearing elements compromised by storm impact
  • Insurance Claim Assistance — Detailed documentation and direct coordination with your insurance provider

After a storm hits, don't wait to assess and address the damage. Exposed areas of your home can deteriorate rapidly with subsequent rain and weather events. Call Becht Pride Restoration and Remodeling for immediate storm damage restoration in Indianapolis. We'll secure your property, work with your insurance, and restore your home to its pre-storm condition.
