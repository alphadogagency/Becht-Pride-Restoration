Trusted Handyman Repair Services in Indianapolis

Every home has a list — that growing collection of small repairs, nagging maintenance tasks, and projects you've been meaning to get to. The door that sticks, the drywall ding from a doorknob, the deck boards that are starting to splinter, the gutters that haven't been cleaned since last fall. Becht Pride Restoration and Remodeling handles all of it. Our professional handyman repair services cover everything from minor fixes to substantial home maintenance projects across Indianapolis and Central Indiana.

Our team of experienced, insured, and bonded technicians show up on time, treat your home with respect, and get the job done right the first time. We live by the motto "Treat every home as if it's your own." That means clean work areas, honest assessments, up-front pricing, and no cutting corners. Too many homeowners have been burned by unreliable contractors, and we're committed to being the exception.

All Becht Pride employees are thoroughly vetted, have passed criminal background checks, and are validated by references and experience. When you let us into your home, you can feel confident that you're working with professionals who take their craft and your trust seriously. Our satisfaction guarantee ensures you'll be happy with the results.

We also handle larger maintenance projects that keep your home in top condition year-round. Power washing your siding, deck, and driveway removes years of dirt and grime and prevents premature deterioration. Gutter cleaning prevents water damage and foundation issues. Window cleaning improves curb appeal and lets more natural light into your home. These aren't glamorous tasks, but they're essential — and we do them all.

Whether you need a single repair or want to knock out an entire list in one visit, our handyman team is ready. We provide free estimates with transparent, up-front pricing so there are never any surprises. Give us a call or fill out the form to get started on your next project.
