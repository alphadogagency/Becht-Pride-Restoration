Storm Damage Restoration for your home

Wind, hail, and heavy rain can leave a home with damaged openings and water inside. Becht Pride provides storm damage restoration in Indianapolis and Central Indiana, connecting emergency protection, cleanup, drying, and repairs.

An exterior loss and an interior leak may be parts of the same project. Tell us about damaged roofing, siding, windows, ceilings, and rooms that became wet. We assess the repair scope and discuss the sequence of work so interior finishes are not repaired before the conditions that damaged them have been addressed.

What’s included

  • Emergency tarping and protection of exposed areas
  • Storm-related debris cleanup and assessment of damage
  • Water extraction and structural drying where needed
  • Repair planning for affected exterior and interior materials
  • Reconstruction of damaged rooms and finishes

Our process

  1. Protect the property

    From a safe location, tell us about exposed areas and water entry. We discuss emergency protection and access to the property.

  2. Assess and clean up

    We document affected materials and plan cleanup and drying where storm water has entered the home.

  3. Complete repairs

    The repair estimate connects the exterior and interior work, including any reconstruction needed after cleanup.

Insurance help and repair estimates

We can document the damage, provide repair estimates, and communicate with your adjuster about the scope of work. Your insurer determines policy coverage, deductibles, and payment. Optional upgrades should be discussed separately from the damage-related repair estimate.

Storm Damage Restoration: common questions

Do you provide emergency tarping?

Yes. Emergency tarping and protection of storm-damaged areas are part of our services. Call to describe the damage and confirm availability and the next step.

Can you handle water damage inside after a storm?

Yes. Water extraction, drying, and interior repairs can be included in the storm restoration plan when the home has water intrusion.

Should I climb onto the roof to check the damage?

Stay clear of damaged roofs, unstable structures, downed power lines, and standing water. Share only observations or photos that can be gathered safely from the ground.

Can storm repairs include remodeling?

Yes. Our team can discuss rebuilding damaged rooms and any separate improvements you want to make. Optional upgrades should be distinguished from the damage-related repair scope.

Will you work with my insurance adjuster?

We can provide damage documentation and repair estimates and communicate about the scope of work. Your insurer determines coverage, deductibles, and claim payment.
