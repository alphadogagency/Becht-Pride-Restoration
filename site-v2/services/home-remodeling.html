Home Remodeling Services in Indianapolis

Whether you're dreaming of a modern kitchen, a spa-like bathroom, or a complete home transformation, Becht Pride Restoration and Remodeling delivers quality remodeling with craftsmanship you can trust. We've been helping Indianapolis homeowners reimagine their living spaces for years, and our reputation is built on honest communication, attention to detail, and results that exceed expectations.

A home remodel is one of the biggest investments you'll make, and choosing the right contractor makes all the difference. At Becht Pride, we treat every home as if it were our own. Our team of experienced, insured, and bonded craftsmen take pride in their work — from the initial design consultation to the final walk-through. We show up on time, keep a clean job site, and communicate openly throughout the entire project.

Our bathroom remodeling services range from simple updates like new vanities and fixtures to full gut renovations with custom tile showers, heated floors, and modern layouts. We handle all the plumbing, electrical, tiling, and finishing work in-house, giving you a single point of contact and a seamless experience. Whether you want to update a powder room or create a luxurious master bath retreat, we'll bring your vision to life.

Kitchen remodels are where families see the biggest impact on their daily lives. We specialize in functional, beautiful kitchen renovations — new cabinetry, countertops, backsplashes, flooring, lighting, and layout reconfiguration. Our team works with you to maximize your space and budget, whether you're refreshing surfaces or doing a complete kitchen overhaul with structural changes.

We also specialize in post-disaster rebuilds. If your home has been damaged by water, fire, storm, or mold, our restoration team handles the mitigation and our remodeling team handles the rebuild. This seamless transition from cleanup to construction is one of the things that sets Becht Pride apart. Many homeowners who come to us after a disaster end up with a home that's better than it was before — because they have the opportunity to redesign and upgrade during the rebuild.

Our Remodeling Services

  • Bathroom Remodels — Custom showers, tub-to-shower conversions, vanities, tile work, and complete bathroom renovations
  • Kitchen Remodels — Cabinetry, countertops, backsplashes, flooring, lighting, and layout changes
  • Full-Home Renovations — Comprehensive remodeling projects that transform your entire living space
  • Post-Disaster Rebuilds — Reconstruction after water, fire, storm, or mold damage
  • Flooring Installation — Hardwood, tile, luxury vinyl plank, and laminate flooring throughout your home
  • Basement Finishing — Converting unfinished basements into functional living spaces
  • Interior Painting & Trim — Fresh paint, new trim, crown molding, and wainscoting
  • Accessibility Modifications — ADA-compliant updates including walk-in showers, grab bars, and wider doorways

Every remodeling project begins with a free consultation where we discuss your goals, timeline, and budget. We provide a detailed, transparent estimate with no hidden fees or surprise charges. Our goal is to make the remodeling process as smooth and stress-free as possible, so you can enjoy the transformation without the headaches. Contact us today to start planning your next project.
