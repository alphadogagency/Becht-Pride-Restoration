Expert Fire Damage Restoration in Indianapolis

A house fire is one of the most devastating events a homeowner can experience. Even after the flames are out, the damage continues — smoke and soot penetrate walls, ceilings, and personal belongings, while water from firefighting efforts creates its own set of problems. Becht Pride Restoration and Remodeling provides comprehensive fire damage restoration in Indianapolis and throughout Central Indiana, helping families recover and rebuild after a fire.

Our fire restoration process begins the moment you call. We coordinate with the fire marshal to gain access to your property as soon as it's safe, then immediately begin securing the structure with board-up and tarping services to prevent further damage from weather, vandalism, or animals. From there, our experienced team assesses the full scope of damage and develops a detailed restoration plan.

Smoke and soot damage can be deceptively widespread. Even areas of your home that weren't directly touched by flames may have significant smoke residue, odor, and discoloration. Our technicians use specialized cleaning agents, thermal fogging, ozone treatments, and hydroxyl generators to eliminate smoke odors and remove soot from every surface — including inside HVAC systems, behind walls, and within personal contents.

Water damage from firefighting is another critical concern that many homeowners don't anticipate. Fire hoses can leave thousands of gallons of water in your home, leading to structural damage, mold growth, and further deterioration if not addressed quickly. Our water extraction and drying equipment handles this secondary damage as part of our comprehensive fire restoration service.

We understand that dealing with insurance after a fire is overwhelming. Our team documents every detail of the damage with photos, moisture readings, and itemized inventories. We work directly with your insurance adjuster, providing the documentation they need to process your claim efficiently. You focus on your family — we handle the rest.

Our Fire Damage Restoration Services

  • Emergency Board-Up & Tarping — Securing your property immediately to prevent additional damage
  • Water Removal & Drying — Extracting water left by firefighting efforts and drying affected areas
  • Soot & Smoke Cleanup — Professional removal of soot, char, and smoke residue from all surfaces
  • Odor Elimination — Advanced deodorization using thermal fogging, ozone, and hydroxyl technology
  • Content Cleaning & Restoration — Salvaging and restoring furniture, clothing, documents, and valuables
  • HVAC Cleaning — Removing smoke particles from ductwork and HVAC systems
  • Structural Repairs & Rebuilding — Complete reconstruction from framing to finishing
  • Insurance Coordination — Direct communication with your insurance company throughout the process

After a fire, time is critical. Soot becomes harder to clean the longer it sits, and smoke odors can become permanently embedded in porous materials. Contact Becht Pride Restoration and Remodeling immediately for professional fire damage restoration. We respond quickly, work efficiently, and rebuild your home with the quality craftsmanship you deserve.
