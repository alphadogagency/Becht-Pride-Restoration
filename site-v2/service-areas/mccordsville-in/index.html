Restoration for McCordsville's changing mix of homes

McCordsville's comprehensive plan distinguishes Old Town, central neighborhoods, boundary neighborhoods, and the town center around McCord Square. That mix is a reminder to plan repairs around the individual house rather than a single description of the town. Becht Pride offers restoration and remodeling for homeowners who need water cleanup, fire and smoke repairs, mold remediation, or help after storm damage.

A newer house can still experience a supply-line or appliance leak, while an established home may have several layers of previous renovation behind a damaged surface. Tell us the age of the affected finishes if you know it, what rooms connect to the area, and when the damage first appeared. Those details help us discuss the assessment and the work needed to restore the space.

Water extraction and structural drying may be followed by replacement drywall, flooring, trim, or cabinets. A fire loss can require smoke cleanup beyond the room where the fire began. Storm damage can connect exterior repairs with moisture inside. Our restoration and remodeling services let you plan those stages together and separate necessary repairs from any kitchen, bathroom, or layout changes you want to add.
