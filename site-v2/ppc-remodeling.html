How long does a tub to shower conversion take?

Timing depends on your design, tile selections and any plumbing changes. We’ll discuss a realistic schedule with your estimate and keep you informed throughout the project.

Do you handle kitchen remodels from start to finish?

Yes. We coordinate cabinetry, countertops, backsplashes, flooring, lighting and layout changes with one point of contact for the project.

Yes. We help turn unfinished basements into functional living space with framing, drywall, flooring, lighting and trim. We also discuss moisture concerns before finishing the space.
