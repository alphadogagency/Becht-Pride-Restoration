Landing page privacy notice

Draft for review. This notice must be reviewed and completed by the business before these landing pages are used for advertising.

This draft describes the three Becht Pride campaign landing pages for remodeling, restoration and insurance repairs. It does not describe every feature of the main website.

Calling the business

The landing pages provide telephone links to (463) 238-4357. Selecting a call button opens your device’s calling application. These pages do not contain a contact form. Information you choose to provide during a call is handled by the business; its call recording, retention and information-sharing practices still need to be confirmed for this notice.

Website hosting

The public website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Hosting and security services may process technical request information, such as an IP address, browser information and the URL requested, to deliver and protect the website. The business should confirm its actual logging and retention settings before approving this notice.

Advertising and call measurement

The draft landing-page code does not load a Google Ads, Google Analytics or CallRail tracking script. It prepares call-button events within the current page for a future approved tracking setup. If advertising tags, forwarding numbers, recording or a consent tool are enabled, this notice must be updated to describe the actual providers, purposes, choices and retention practices.

Questions

For questions about Becht Pride’s information practices, call (463) 238-4357. The final notice should also include the business’s approved privacy contact and any applicable rights-request instructions.
